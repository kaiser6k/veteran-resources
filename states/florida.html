Florida

Property tax exemptions, no state income tax, education waivers, employment preference, vehicle, and recreation perks for veterans.

Quick wins: No state income tax. $5,000 property tax exemption at 10%+ disability; full homestead exemption at 100% P&T. Free lifetime state park pass if you have a service-connected disability. Out-of-state tuition waived at public colleges.

Housing & Property Tax

$5,000 Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption (Fla. Stat. 196.24)

Resident veterans with a VA-certified service-connected disability of 10% or greater get a $5,000 reduction in the assessed value of their property. Unremarried surviving spouses who were married at the time of death also qualify.

  • Apply through your county property appraiser's office.
  • Bring your VA disability rating letter and DD-214.

Full Homestead Exemption for 100% P&T (Fla. Stat. 196.081)

Veterans honorably discharged with a service-connected total and permanent disability are completely exempt from all ad valorem property taxes on their homestead. Surviving spouses carry it over until remarriage or sale.

Combat-Disabled Veteran Discount Age 65+ (Fla. Stat. 196.082)

Veterans 65 or older with a combat-related, permanent service-connected disability get a homestead tax discount equal to their disability rating percentage (e.g., 50% rating = 50% discount). Apply to the county property appraiser by March 1.

Deployed Military Property Tax Exemption

Service members deployed outside the U.S. during the previous calendar year get a property tax exemption scaled to the share of the year spent deployed.

Source: Florida Department of Veterans' Affairs and Florida Statutes. Confirm with your county property appraiser.

Income Tax

Florida has no state income tax. Military retirement pay, VA disability compensation, SBP, and TSP distributions are entirely exempt — no filing or election required at the state level.

Education

Congressman C.W. Bill Young Veteran Tuition Waiver (Fla. Stat. 1009.26)

Honorably discharged veterans, Reservists, or National Guard members who physically reside in Florida while enrolled get out-of-state tuition and fees waived at all Florida public colleges, universities, and career centers — reduced to in-state rates. Can save $10,000–$20,000+ per year.

  • Also extends to spouses and dependent children residing in Florida who are using GI Bill benefits.
  • Active duty members stationed or residing outside Florida also qualify for the out-of-state fee waiver.

Purple Heart & Combat Decoration Tuition Waiver

Undergraduate tuition waived at Florida state universities, Florida College System institutions, and career/technical centers for recipients of the Purple Heart or superior combat decorations (capped at 110% of required program hours).

Scholarships for Children & Spouses of Deceased or Disabled Veterans

Dependent children and spouses of veterans who died from a service-connected disability or who are 100% P&T disabled may receive tuition waivers and scholarships at Florida public institutions.

  • Apply via floridastudentfinancialaid.org or call 888-827-2004.

Priority Course Registration

Florida public institutions that offer priority registration must give it to veterans receiving GI Bill educational benefits (Fla. Stat. 1004.075).

Employment

Veterans' Preference in Public Employment (Fla. Stat. 295.07)

The state and all political subdivisions give preference in appointment, promotion after deployment, and retention. Eligible groups include disabled veterans, wartime veterans, unremarried surviving spouses, spouses of totally disabled veterans, and current Guard/Reserve members.

  • On exam-scored jobs: +20 points (disabled vets & qualifying spouses), +15 (wartime vets), +10 (peacetime vets/Guard/Reserve).
  • Disabled vets rated 30%+ with the 20-point preference go to the top of the register.
  • State agencies must develop written veterans' recruitment plans with annual goals.
  • Postsecondary education requirements may be waived for qualified veterans and service members.

CareerSource Florida

Veterans receive priority of service for training, employment, and placement through CareerSource Florida's network of 24 local workforce boards and 100+ career centers. Employment specialists with veteran backgrounds are available at many locations.

Vehicle & Recreation

Free Disabled Veteran License Plate & Registration

100% disabled veterans get a free disabled veteran license plate, free vehicle registration, and sales tax exemption on one vehicle. Apply through your local tax collector/DMV with your VA rating letter and DD-214.

State Parks Pass Discounts (Fla. Stat. 258.0145)

  • Any honorably discharged veteran or active duty member: 25% off annual entrance passes.
  • Veterans with a service-connected disability: free lifetime family annual entrance pass.
  • Surviving spouses and parents of service members killed in combat: free lifetime family pass.

Apply online at FloridaStateParks.com or at any park ranger station.

How to Get Help

Florida Department of Veterans' Affairs

Free benefits counseling with a State Veterans' Service Officer. Call 727-319-7440 (they'll return your call within 24 hours) or email FDVA.VSO@FDVA.FL.GOV.

  • Florida Veterans Support Line: 1-844-MyFLVet (1-844-693-5838) or dial 211
  • Headquarters: 11351 Ulmerton Road, Suite 311-K, Largo, FL 33778 · 727-518-3202
  • Benefits & Assistance Division: 9500 Bay Pines Blvd., Suite 214, Bay Pines, FL 33744
  • County Veteran Service Offices (CVSOs) across the state — free, accredited counselors.

Official site: floridavets.org · Annual Florida Veterans' Benefits Guide available there.

New Hampshire

Property tax credits, no state income tax, education waivers, employment preference, and recreation perks for veterans.

Quick wins: No state income tax on military retirement or VA disability. Property tax credits from $51 up to $5,000 depending on your town and rating. Free lifetime hunting/fishing license if you're 100% P&T.

Housing & Property Tax

Wartime Veteran Property Tax Credit (RSA 72:28)

Wartime veterans, their spouses, or surviving spouses may qualify for a property tax credit of $51 to $750 per year. Municipalities can set their own amount above the $51 state minimum.

  • Requires at least 90 days of active duty during a qualifying war or conflict, honorable discharge (or separation due to service-connected disability).
  • File form PA-29 with your town's assessing office by April 15, after one year of NH residency.

All Veterans' Tax Credit (RSA 72:28-b)

Optional credit for any NH-resident veteran with at least 90 days of active duty and an honorable discharge, regardless of wartime service. Amount matches what your town adopted for the wartime credit ($50 up to $750).

Disabled Veteran Property Tax Credit (RSA 72:35)

$700 credit on your primary residence if you're permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected condition, a double amputee, or paraplegic. Towns can vote to raise it as high as $5,000.

  • Extends to unremarried surviving spouses.
  • Full property tax exemption exists for qualifying veterans in a VA-adapted specially adapted homestead (RSA 72:36-a).

Surviving Spouse Credit (RSA 72:29-a)

Surviving spouse of a veteran killed on active duty may qualify for a credit of $700 to $2,000 (up to $2,500 under legislation effective April 2027).

Source: NH Department of Military Affairs and Veterans Services. Amounts depend on your municipality — confirm with your local assessor.

Income Tax

New Hampshire has no state income tax on earned income, wages, or retirement pay. Military retirement and VA disability compensation are completely tax-free at the state level — no filing required.

Education

National Guard Tuition Assistance

NH National Guard members may attend state-supported postsecondary institutions tuition-free on a space-available basis after exhausting federal educational entitlements (Post-9/11 GI Bill, etc.).

  • Must be an active sworn member residing in or out of state.
  • Can transfer the benefit to a spouse after completing initial 6-year service obligation.
  • Army Guard Education Office: 603-225-1207 · Air Guard Force Development: 603-430-3529

Free Tuition for Children of Disabled Veterans (RSA 187-A:20)

Children of a veteran who is totally and permanently disabled from a service-connected condition get free tuition at University System of New Hampshire institutions (UNH, Plymouth, Keene, etc.).

  • Child must be under 27 on the first day of the term.
  • Parent must have been domiciled in NH at entry to service or when rated disabled.
  • Must exhaust federal GI Bill benefits first and file FAFSA.

Free Tuition + Scholarship for Children of Deceased Wartime Veterans (RSA 193:19-21)

Children (ages 16–25) of a wartime veteran who died on active duty or from a service-connected disability get free tuition at NH public colleges plus up to $2,500/year for room, board, books, and supplies (up to 4 years, based on financial need).

Employment

Veterans' Hiring Preference (RSA 283:4, 275-G)

Veterans, unremarried surviving spouses, and spouses of disabled veterans receive preference in public departments and public works of the state and local units. Additional named preferences exist at the Liquor Commission, Division of Veterans Services, and Veterans Home.

  • As of August 2025, private employers may adopt written preference policies covering veterans, active-duty members, and military spouses.
  • Disabled veteran-owned and military spouse-owned businesses get a state procurement preference.

Priority of Service at NH Employment Services

Veterans and eligible spouses get priority for employment, training, and placement services at NH Works career centers and all state workforce programs funded by the Department of Labor.

Vehicle & Recreation

Free Registration & Special Plates (RSA 261:86)

No registration fee and free special plates for one vehicle owned by a veteran who is an amputee, paraplegic, or rated permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected condition. Free driver's license also available for qualifying amputees/paraplegics with a VA-provided vehicle.

Free Perpetual Hunting & Fishing License (RSA 214:13)

Honorably discharged NH-resident veterans who are permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected disability get a free lifetime Fish and Game license. One-time $10 administrative fee on application.

  • Paraplegic veterans may hunt from motor vehicles or boats with a proper permit (RSA 207:7-a).
  • Free fishing permits for VA Medical Center patients in Manchester and NH Veterans' Home residents.

Veteran Decal Plates & Parking

Distinctive military and veteran-themed plates plus a multi-use Veteran Decal (obtained through the Division of Veterans Services, not DMV). Vehicles with disabled-veteran accessibility plates get free parking time in any city or town.

How to Get Help

NH Division of Veterans Services

Free benefits counseling with a Veterans Service Officer (VSO). Schedule at 603-624-9230 or toll-free 1-800-622-9230.

  • Office: 275 Chestnut Street, Room 517, Manchester, NH 03101
  • VSO sites in North Conway, Portsmouth, Tilton, and others — all by appointment.
  • Main Dept. of Military Affairs & Veterans Services: 1 Minuteman Way, Concord, NH 03301 · 603-225-1200

Official site: nhveterans.nh.gov

Veteran discounts

These are the national programs vets actually use. Amounts change, and some stores cap savings. Verify with ID.me, SheerID, or a veteran ID before you count on the number.

Home and hardware

10% · cap $400/year

Home Depot

Everyday discount for veterans, active duty, and spouses after you enroll in the app and scan the QR code. Resets each January.

10% everyday

Lowe's

Eligible purchases in store and online after ID.me. Often includes MyLowe's Silver perks and free delivery over $25.

15% in store

Sherwin-Williams

Paint and supplies with a military or veteran ID. Ask at the counter.

Outdoor and gear

10% most items

Bass Pro Shops

About 10% off most products, often 5% on firearms and ammo. Boats and some big-ticket items are excluded.

10% most items

Cabela's

Same program as Bass Pro. Bring ID in store or verify online.

20% online

Under Armour

Usually 20% at Brand House / online and 10% at Factory House after ID.me.

10%

Nike

Online and in many stores after SheerID.

Tech and phone

10%

Apple

Eligible products and accessories after ID.me.

plan discount

Verizon / AT&T / T-Mobile

Each carrier runs a military plan. Verify once with ID.me.

~10%

Dell and Microsoft

PCs and accessories through their military storefronts.

Food

often 10%

Texas Roadhouse

Many locations take 10% year-round. Franchise rules vary — ask first.

Veterans Day

Applebee's, Chili's, Olive Garden

Free or discounted meals on November 11 from a limited menu.

ask first

Local diners

A lot of restaurants still discount with a veteran ID. Never assume.

Parks and tickets

free lifetime

National Parks Military Pass

Veterans and Gold Star families get a free America the Beautiful lifetime pass. Get it in person or through Recreation.gov / USGS.

request tickets

Vet Tix

Free or cheap seats at games, concerts, and shows.

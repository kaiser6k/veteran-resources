Housing help

Two different problems get mixed together: needing a roof this week, and wanting an adapted or mortgage-free house later. Start with the first call if you are on the street or about to be.

Need a bed tonight? National Call Center for Homeless Veterans: 877-424-3838 (24/7). Or dial 211. You do not have to already have VA health care.

VA programs that actually house people

Short-term rent

SSVF

Supportive Services for Veteran Families. A local nonprofit gets the VA grant and can pay deposits, short-term rent, and utilities if you are homeless or about to be. This is the fastest nonprofit door in most cities.

Find an SSVF grantee

Permanent voucher

HUD-VASH

A Housing Choice Voucher from HUD plus a VA case manager. Built for veterans coming out of homelessness. Apply through the VA homeless program and your public housing authority.

HUD-VASH

Transitional

Grant and Per Diem

Community nonprofits run beds and service centers under a VA grant. Good when you need a place to stabilize before a voucher or lease.

GPD overview

Adapt a house

VA housing grants

SAH, SHA, and HISA help pay to buy, build, or change a home if you have certain service-connected disabilities. Separate from the free-home charities below.

Disability housing grants

Nonprofits that build or give homes

Adapted, mortgage-free

Homes For Our Troops

Builds custom accessible homes for severely injured post-9/11 veterans. No cost to the veteran. Usually wants an SAH rating in the packet. Start with their inquiry form, not a cold call to a local chapter.

hfotusa.org/inquiry

Smart homes + homeless

Tunnel to Towers

Smart Home Program for catastrophically injured veterans and first responders. Separate Homeless Veteran Program can cover hotels, deposits, and case management in most states.

t2t.org

Mortgage-free gift

Building Homes for Heroes

Gifts homes to injured veterans, Gold Star families, and some first responders. Application, not a walk-in. Waitlists are real.

buildinghomesforheroes.org

Adapted smart homes

Gary Sinise Foundation R.I.S.E.

Mortgage-free adapted homes for the most severely wounded veterans and first responders. Highly selective. Worth applying if that is your situation; not a general housing waitlist.

R.I.S.E. program

Repairs + ownership

Habitat for Humanity Veterans Build

Local Habitat affiliates build with veterans, do critical repairs, and offer affordable mortgages. This is sweat-equity homeownership, not a free house. Call the affiliate in your county.

habitat.org/veteran

Accessibility mods

Purple Heart Homes

Builds and modifies homes for service-connected disabled veterans. Smaller than HFOT. Still a real option if you need ramps, baths, or a livable first floor.

purplehearthomesusa.org

Nonprofits that keep you in a rental

Local intake

SSVF grantee in your city

These are the nonprofits most vets actually use: rent, deposits, landlord talks, case management. The name changes by county. 877-424-3838 or 211 will route you to the current grantee.

National network

U.S.VETS

One of the larger veteran-specific housing nonprofits. Permanent supportive housing and services in multiple states. Check whether they operate near you.

usvets.org

National network

Volunteers of America

Runs veteran housing and GPD programs in a lot of cities. Same idea: local program, national name.

voa.org/veterans

Directory

National Coalition for Homeless Veterans

Not a landlord. A map of service providers if 211 and the VA line bounce you around.

nchv.org

SSVF intake

211 / First Contact

Tampa Bay screens veterans for SSVF and sends people to St. Vincent de Paul Cares. Call 211, or text HousingFirst to 898211.

firstcontact.org

SSVF + shelter

St. Vincent de Paul Cares

Major Tampa Bay SSVF provider. Also opened a dedicated veteran shelter in North Tampa in 2026 after taking over a foreclosed site. Meals and wraparound help while they look for a lease.

SSVF

Tampa Crossroads

Veterans Assistance Center runs SSVF. Phone pre-screen. You can qualify even without VA health care.

tampacrossroads.com

Permanent supportive housing in Tampa, including veteran-designated units. Ask about the veteran duplex and scattered-site apartments. 813-237-4907.

actsfl.org

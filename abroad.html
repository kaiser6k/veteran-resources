Living outside the United States

Moving abroad does not cancel the benefits you earned. Most VA payments still go through. Health care overseas is narrower than stateside VA care — mainly service-connected conditions.

What still follows you

VA says most benefits are payable no matter where you live, including disability compensation, pension, education, insurance you already have, VR&E, and burial benefits.

Keep a current physical address on file. If VA only has a mail drop, claim exams and letters get delayed or missed.

Health care: Foreign Medical Program

FMP is not full VA health insurance. It pays for medically necessary care in a foreign country when that care is for a VA-rated service-connected disability, a condition that makes that disability worse, or care you need for VR&E (non-service-connected VR&E care needs a case-manager referral).

You can usually pick any licensed local provider. Covered items can include office visits, hospital stays, emergency care, physical therapy, FDA-approved prescriptions, and prescribed equipment — when they treat the service-connected condition.

FMP does not cover everyday care unrelated to your rating, care or supplies from the U.S. or U.S. territories, or mail-order medicine shipped from the U.S.

Show providers your FMP benefits letter (VA.gov → your name → Letters). File claims within two years of the care or hospital discharge. If the clinic will not bill VA, pay first and file for reimbursement.

Philippines and Canada

The only VA clinic in a foreign country is the Manila VA Outpatient Clinic. If you live in the Philippines and have a service-connected rating, enroll there and keep FMP for care the clinic cannot do. You need a Philippines address and local phone number.

Canada has often been handled differently from the rest of FMP. Ask FMP or Ask VA before you book care there.

School

You can use GI Bill benefits at a foreign school only if VA approved that program. That is not the same as a U.S. school’s study-abroad semester. Confirm the program — not just the university name — before you enroll.

Housing and money

  • Disability and pension can go by international direct deposit. Treasury does not add a conversion fee; your foreign bank might.
  • A VA home loan cannot buy a house in another country. It only covers the U.S. and territories. Entitlement stays on the books while you live abroad.
  • Some Specially Adapted Housing grants can still apply if you have a real ownership interest and VA approves the work first.

Privacy

This is a static website. There is no login, no newsletter popup, and no ad network. We wrote this so you do not have to guess.

What we do not do

  • We do not set first-party cookies.
  • We do not run Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, or other trackers we control.
  • We do not create accounts or sell lists.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

What still happens when you load a page

The files live on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub can see the request itself — roughly the page you asked for, your IP address, and browser type. That is their infrastructure, not a form we built.

The clickable U.S. map may load a public SVG from a content-delivery network (jsDelivr / GitHub). That request can also show them an IP address. If the map fails, the state dropdown still works.

Links to VA.gov, Home Depot, state agencies, and charities leave this site. Their cookies and rules are theirs.

Email

If you write kaiser6k@gmail.com, that message sits in email. We use it to fix the site, not to build a marketing list. Do not send Social Security numbers, claim files, or medical records.

GDPR / CCPA, in plain words

If you are in the EU, UK, or California: we are not running a profiling business on this site. We do not seek to collect personal data through the pages themselves. Hosting logs at GitHub are the main technical residue of a visit.

This page is a notice, not a lawyer stamp and not a claim that GitHub, Inc. is “GDPR certified” on our behalf. After Service is a veteran-built field guide, not a government site and not a data broker.
